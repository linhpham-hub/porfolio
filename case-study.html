Chapter 1

From reports to running the contests

I started on reporting: monthly KPI, agent-turnover and risk-control reports for the Regional Office, covering 3,000+ agents. I also led the move from Excel reports to Tableau dashboards, which cut preparation time by an estimated 60%.

Then I took on a new challenge: the agency contests and campaigns, running every month alongside quarterly and yearly programs.

Sales-force contestsRewards for agents and team leaders who hit production and activity targets.

Customer contestsPromotions for customers, which give agents a reason to reach out and help them close more sales.

Reports tell the sales force how they did. Contests move them to do more — so they had to be clear, fair and paid on time.

What I take into a data role

  • Start from the question the user actually has — here, "what will I earn?" — and build the report or tool around it.
  • Forecasting is part of delivery: a contest only goes ahead once its cost is projected and fits the approved budget.
  • Cross-functional work runs on one shared timeline: leadership, legal, compliance, procurement, finance, vendors and sales all moved together.
  • A number people can't explain is a number they won't trust — every payout had to be traceable line by line.
