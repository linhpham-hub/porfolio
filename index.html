Project 02

Real-Estate Lead Dashboard

Interactive demoSide project · in progress

A side project I'm building for a friend who works as a lead realtor, to help him run his listings and leads in one place.

His listings are promoted on several property websites at once, so enquiries arrive from many places. The dashboard pulls them together to show leads per day, flags people who enquired more than once or on more than one site, and highlights listings that draw few enquiries — a signal that their photos or description need work.

  • One view of every source: enquiries from all listing websites combined, with leads per day and a breakdown by site.
  • Repeat enquirers: the same person contacting more than once, or through more than one website, is flagged instead of counted twice.
  • Listings that need attention: the least-enquired listings are shown next to the most popular, so their content can be improved.
  • Pipeline and drop reasons: where each lead stands, and why leads are lost.
  • Listings: one place for every property's details, photos, videos and running notes.

The demo runs on invented sample data. No real client, property or lead information is shown, and the live tool itself stays private.

Finnish Vocabulary App

Open to everyoneStill growing
“How can you study Finnish without knowing the vocabulary?”— my Finnish teacher

The Finnish A1.2 course at Metropolia has frequent vocabulary tests, so I built this app from the teacher's glossaries to study for them: 312 words, practised as flashcards, a multiple-choice quiz, or typed answers.

I keep adding new chapters as the course goes on, and share it with classmates who are starting Finnish from scratch, like me.

Open the app
  • Three study modes — flashcards, multiple-choice quiz, and write mode with meaning-based hints instead of first-letter spoilers.
  • Spaced repetition, so missed words come back sooner and known words less often.
  • Works offline and installs on a phone like a normal app.
  • The word list rebuilds itself from the course glossaries whenever a new chapter is added.
